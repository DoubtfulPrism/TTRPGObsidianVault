# Undead Traits (Ex)

**Type:** Ex | **Location:** Immune
**Format:** undead traits
**Source:** [Archives of Nethys](https://www.aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Undead%20Traits) (Bestiary pg. 305)

## Description
- **Immune to:** death effects, disease, mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, morale effects, patterns, phantasms), paralysis, poison, sleep, stun, and any effect requiring a Fortitude save (unless it works on objects or is harmless).
- **Not subject to:** ability drain, energy drain, or nonlethal damage.
- **Immune to damage/penalties to physical ability scores:** Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution, as well as fatigue and exhaustion.
- **Not at risk of:** death from massive damage.
