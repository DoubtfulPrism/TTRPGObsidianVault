# Construct Traits (Ex)

**Type:** Ex | **Location:** Immune
**Format:** construct traits
**Source:** [Archives of Nethys](https://www.aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Construct%20Traits) (Bestiary pg. 300)

## Description
- **Immune to:** all mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, morale effects, patterns, phantasms), bleed, disease, death effects, necromancy effects, paralysis, poison, sleep effects, and stunning.
- **Not subject to:** nonlethal damage, ability damage, ability drain, fatigue, exhaustion, energy drain, or any effect requiring a Fortitude save (unless it works on objects or is harmless).
- **Not at risk of:** death from massive damage. Immediately destroyed when reduced to 0 hit points or less.
- **Healing & Raising:** Cannot heal damage on their own (typically repaired via *make whole* or Craft), and cannot be raised or resurrected.
- **Other:** Do not breathe, eat, or sleep (unless specifically noted).
