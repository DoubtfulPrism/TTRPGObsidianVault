# Campaign Setup

> [!tip] New to Obsidian or this vault? Start with the Demo!
> Before you begin taking notes for your campaign, take a few minutes to explore the **`00-Start Here/`** folder:
> - Read the [[00-Start Here/How-To Guide|How-To Guide]] for a quick walkthrough of templates, `[[wikilinks]]`, embedding monster traits, and tracking plot threads.
> - Open the [[Demo - Dashboard|Demo Dashboard]] and click through the sample sessions, PCs, NPCs, and monsters to see how everything fits together.
>
> You can keep `00-Start Here/` around as a reference or delete it whenever you're comfortable—your live [[Campaign-Vault/Dashboard|Campaign Dashboard]] already ignores it.

## Before Session 1
- [ ] Read the [[00-Start Here/How-To Guide|How-To Guide]] and browse the `00-Start Here/` demo notes.
- [ ] Open [[Campaign-Vault/Dashboard|Campaign Dashboard]] and fill in the **Current Context** box (Adventure Path name and starting location).
- [ ] Create your party's PC notes (right-click `02-Codex/02.03 PCs` → **New note**, or press `Ctrl/Cmd + N`, then `Ctrl/Cmd + P` → *Templater: Open Insert Template modal* and choose **PC Template**).
