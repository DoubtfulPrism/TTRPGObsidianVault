# Goblin

## Observed Details
- **Attacks:** Shortbows, dogslicers (small slashing), sometimes alchemist's fire.
- **Defenses / Resistances:** High AC/Reflex for their size; low Will saves.
- **Weaknesses:** Easily panicked when outnumbered or when their leader drops.
- **Notes:** Hate horses and dogs.
