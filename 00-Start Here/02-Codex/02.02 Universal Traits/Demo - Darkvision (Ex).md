# Darkvision (Ex)

**Type:** Ex | **Location:** Senses
**Format:** darkvision 60 ft.
**Source:** [Archives of Nethys](https://www.aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Darkvision) (Bestiary pg. 298)

## Description
A creature with darkvision can see in total darkness out to the listed range (usually 60 ft.). Darkvision is black and white only, but otherwise works like normal sight.
