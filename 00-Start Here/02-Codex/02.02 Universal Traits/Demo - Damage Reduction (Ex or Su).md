# Damage Reduction (Ex or Su)

**Type:** Ex / Su | **Location:** Defensive Abilities
**Format:** DR 5/bludgeoning (or similar)
**Source:** [Archives of Nethys](https://www.aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Damage%20Reduction) (Bestiary pg. 299)

## Description
Ignores the listed amount of damage from most weapons and natural attacks unless the attack meets the bypass condition (e.g., `bludgeoning`, `slashing`, `magic`, `cold iron`, `silver`). Energy damage, spells, and spell-like abilities bypass DR.
